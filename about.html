INSIDE TOP CHEF.

Chinese and kebab takeaway on Wootton Road — a small kitchen quietly feeding the neighbourhood evening after evening.

7 WOOTTON ROAD

Top Chef sits at number seven on Wootton Road, between Gaywood roundabout and the houses that run down toward Lynnsport. It's not a destination restaurant — it's the kind of takeaway you pass on the way home from work, the one whose phone number ends up in everyone's contacts.

The kitchen runs two disciplines side by side. A Cantonese wok turns out crispy duck, lemon chicken, sweet-and-sour and the kind of stir-fried noodles that come out hissing. A kebab grill on the other side handles doner, shish and chicken kebabs — pitta wrapped in foil, chilli sauce optional.

Most orders are phoned in. The counter is small, the bags go out warm, and the receipt prints with a name on it because the staff write it down rather than ask twice.

How to order

Call first.

The order line is the quickest way in. Tell us what you'd like, give us your name, and we'll have it bagged at the counter or on its way to your door. Card and cash both fine at the counter.

We're an evening kitchen — call us when the wok is on. If you need a delivery time, the phone is the place to confirm.
