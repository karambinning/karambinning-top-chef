What we do

Two kitchens. One counter.

Top Chef has been feeding the Wootton Road end of Gaywood for years — a small kitchen that runs a Cantonese wok and a kebab grill side by side. Chinese classics on one half of the menu, doner and shish on the other, all out of the same door.

Pick up on the way home, or call ahead and we'll have it bagged for you. The order line is always the quickest route in.
